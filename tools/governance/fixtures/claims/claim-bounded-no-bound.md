# Fixture: a bound-carrying verb with no bound

GOV-3-03 and GOV-3-04. `bounded` and `exhaustively checked` are exactly the verbs
whose meaning is the envelope they hold within; stated without one they read as
unqualified strength.

Exploration is bounded.

The dining model is exhaustively checked.
