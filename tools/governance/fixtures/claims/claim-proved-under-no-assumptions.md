# Fixture: `proved under` that names no assumptions

GOV-3-05. The verb is the controlled one, but the assumptions it is proved under
are missing, so the claim cannot be sized by a reader.

The refinement theorem is proved under.

Agreement is proved under assumptions.

Termination is proved under certain conditions.
