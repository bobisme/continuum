# RFC 0037: Intent Contract Schema and Policy

## Status
Draft.

## Schema
Claims, assumptions, observers, scope, bounds, fault model, fairness/time, trust/opaque boundaries, assurance, hard/soft objectives, non-vacuity, change policy.

## Identity
Canonical encoding and content hash. Human labels are metadata; semantic IDs derive from AST/config.

## Policy
Field-level capabilities and review rules. Default repair profile denies protected changes.

## Diff
Every revision records classified semantic relation, invalidated evidence, and unresolved obligations.

## Acceptance
Round-trip schema, deterministic identity, implication tests for finite fragment, and adversarial mutation suite.
