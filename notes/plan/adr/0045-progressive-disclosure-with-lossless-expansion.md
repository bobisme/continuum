# ADR 0045: Progressive Disclosure with Lossless Expansion

## Status
Accepted.

## Context
Formal tools either overwhelm newcomers or hide critical assumptions behind simplified badges.

## Decision
Default views are concise, but every summary item links to exact semantic artifacts. Simplification includes omissions, assurance, and expansion paths. Human personas share one evidence model.

## Consequences
- more presentation/query work;
- no separate “simple semantics” mode;
- explanations can be empirically evaluated;
- exact evidence remains reachable.

## Evidence required
Task studies measuring diagnosis and assurance calibration.
