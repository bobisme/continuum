## Promotion contract

Every research track must produce:

1. a formal claim and explicit property/observer scope;
2. a state-of-the-art baseline;
3. an implementation artifact and reproducible corpus;
4. a preservation proof, translation validator, or sharply bounded claim;
5. preregistered success and failure metrics;
6. adversarial semantic mutants;
7. a promote, continue, or delete decision.

No research prototype silently changes stable CML, CIR, domain-pack, or proof-receipt semantics.
